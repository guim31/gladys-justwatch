## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added

- One device per list of streaming platforms (up to three, films and/or
  series): titles arrived today, in 7 days, and the latest arrival, read from
  JustWatch in the chosen country (France by default, 14 countries).
- Watched titles: a device counting those available on your platforms, and the
  scene trigger "watched title available".
- Scene trigger "new title on my platforms", filtered by list and kind.
- Scene action "where to watch a title" (live search, outputs usable in
  conditions).
- Dashboard widget "JustWatch new titles": latest arrivals of 1, 3 or 7 days,
  and where the watched titles are.
- Texts and documentation in English, French, German and Spanish.
